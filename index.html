WELCOME TO NEAPOLITAN STATION

Hi. You found the page I built instead of going to bed.

NEAPOLITAN STATION is a small pink-and-chocolate corner of Neocities where I keep the things I like: soft colours, old cameras, warm drinks, cats that sleep on my keyboard, and web pages that somebody wrote out by hand instead of generating.

Everything here is written in a text editor — no build step, no framework, no cookie banner, no machine deciding what you look at next. There's a guestbook, a shelf of recipes, and a links page full of places I actually visit.

View source all you like. It's commented, unminified, and yours to learn from.

ABOUT ME

I'm lallaguntgfvr — Lalla is fine, or whatever nickname you feel like typing. I make things in pink, I keep a notebook of website ideas I'll never get to, and I think a homepage should be somewhere you can visit rather than something you scroll past.

I learned HTML by viewing source on other people's Neocities pages, which is exactly the right way to learn it. If that's what you're doing right now: hello, keep going, nobody is grading you.

welcome.txt

Hi. You found the little website I built instead of scrolling.

This page is a hand-written pile of HTML in a plain text editor, uploaded to Neocities for the price of zero dollars. No build step, no bundle, no framework, no cookie banner, no machine deciding what you should look at next. Just a page, a guestbook, and a hit counter that I'm honest about being fake.

The best websites are the ones somebody made on purpose, badly, and left up anyway.

— a post I read at 3am and think about constantly
  • Look around. Everything here is hand-made or hand-picked.
  • Sign the guestbook. It saves to your browser, so you're really only writing to yourself. That still counts.
  • Take a button. There is a 88×31 badge at the bottom with copy-paste code and everything.

Grabbed a piece of code you liked? View source. It's unminified, commented, and yours to learn from. That's the whole point of this corner of the web.

about.me

I'm lallaguntgfvr — Lalla for short, if you like, or whatever else you'd rather type. I make pixel art badly and with great joy, I keep a text file of website ideas I'll never build, and I believe a homepage should be a place you can visit rather than a feed you scroll.

I learned HTML from viewing source on other people's Neocities pages, which is exactly how it should be learned. If you're doing the same thing right now: hello, keep going, the table-based layout era is a museum, not a jail.

I like old computers, small search engines, dial-up noises, the sound of a mechanical keyboard at 2am, and the specific feeling of finishing a page nobody asked for.

Why this site exists

Because the personal homepage is the best idea the web ever had and we let it get buried under algorithmic sludge. This one is small and neon-coloured and entirely unoptimised for engagement. If it loads in a second and makes one person want to make their own, it did its job.

gallery

Rendered with nearest-neighbour scaling instead of smoothing, which is why they stay crisp when the box grows. Never let a browser blur your pixels without a fight.
